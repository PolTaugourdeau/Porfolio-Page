About Me

I'm Pol Taugourdeau, a 3D generalist artist with a passion for creating worlds, characters and assets that come to life in video games, film and advertising.

From hard surface modeling to level design, I combine technical precision with artistic vision. I've worked on personal projects, indie studios and professional collaborations that have given me a complete view of the production pipeline.

Currently open to new opportunities. I share my work on ArtStation and Instagram and keep publishing projects on itch.io.
